Time It — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

The short version: Time It collects no data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking — and it never connects to a server. Everything the app knows stays on your devices.

Data we collect

None. Time It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and makes no network requests. Under Apple's App Privacy classification, Time It is "Data Not Collected."

What stays on your device

  • Timers and settings. Your timer presets, colors, rest durations, and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch. They sync between your own two devices using Apple's encrypted Watch Connectivity — they never pass through any server of ours.
  • Health data (Apple Watch). With your permission, Time It reads your heart rate during workouts to display it on screen, and records workout timers and sessions to Apple's Health app. This data is handled entirely by Apple's HealthKit on your device. Time It never transmits health data anywhere, never stores it outside Apple Health, and cannot see it unless a workout or timer is running. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health, and you can turn off workout recording per timer inside the app.
  • Notifications. Timer alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device by iOS. They don't involve any server.

Third parties

There are none. Time It shares data with no third parties, because it has no data and no network connection to share it over.

Children

Time It is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Time It ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised before that version ships.
