How Come website, MA Design show 2012

How Come, Goldsmiths MA Design show 2012, was a part of the London Design Festival. I collaborated with Andrew Welch in creating the website for the final MA show; 'How Come' Creating and coordinating the How Come site was a fun opportunity which helped me get to know all the people in the course and their projects.

My design was inspired from the logo created by Pete Moffat, and the MA group decision to use stamps instead of business cards for the show.

Project finished on September, 2012
