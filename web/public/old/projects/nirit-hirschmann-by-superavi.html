Nirit Hirschmann

Nirit Hirschmann is a makeup artist. Her website is a portfolio website where she gathers her works and special projects, displaying them under different categories. Nirit's website was created in collaboration with Moo&Ar Studio. Moo&Ar studios hired my services and were in charge of the design, cataloging, working with the client, and I was in charge of all technical issues including: platform, interaction, SEO.

Nirit's website has a very minimal design in order to direct the focus to the subjects, the models in her works. In each category the works are presented in a flowing display for the user to enjoy.

Project finished on May, 2010
