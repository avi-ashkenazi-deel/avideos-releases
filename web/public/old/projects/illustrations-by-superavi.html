Illustrations

In my illustrations, I try to fool around with reality, distort perspectives, and experiment with materials and code. I do a lot of "legal" graffiti, sketching weird things and coding for fun. Right now I'm working on some designs for t-shirts, and I hope I'll upload some soon.

Once in a while I DJ and VJ at parties, usually Hip-Hop or Dub-Step parties, where I have a great opportunity to see some of my work in motion.
