Blood Earth app

Blood Earth is the worlds best dub, dubstep and reggae remix app, born from a collaboration between Delhi Sultanate (New-Delhi), Jazzsteppa (London/ Berlin), Atomique (Vienna) & Zvulon dub soundsystem (Tel-Aviv).

The app lets you remix and breakdown 8 versions of the original Blood Earth tune, creating endless new combinations.

This app is part of the Word Sound Power project. A goethe Institue funded program that aims to expose the injustices caused by global corporations in rural India.
