# Brand First Principles — Draft v1

**Status:** Draft for team review. Written in the "craft at speed" voice (see ../POSITIONING.md).
**Format:** Each principle = a declarative title + 2–3 sentences. Small, sharp, load-bearing. These are Tier 1 — the few things we say carefully.

> These are illustrative drafts to react to, not final language. The point is the *shape and voice*; the team owns the actual beliefs.

---

### 1. Speed is a material, not an excuse

We ship faster than almost anywhere. That's not a reason for worse design — it's the material we work in. We design *for* the pace instead of complaining about it.

### 2. The complexity is the job

Payroll, compliance, employing people in a hundred countries — it's genuinely hard, and it doesn't get simpler because we wish it would. Our job isn't to hide the complexity. It's to make the person on the other side feel like it was simple all along.

### 3. Ship, then sharpen

Real craft in front of real people this week beats perfect craft in front of no one next quarter. We'd rather learn from something live than polish something theoretical. The sharpening is continuous — it just happens in the open.

### 4. Opinions over options

A good default beats a settings page. Every toggle we add is a decision we pushed onto the user. We make the call, and we own it.

### 5. We design the boring parts

The most-used screens are almost never the glamorous ones. The list view, the empty state, the error, the tenth step of onboarding — that's where the care actually shows, so that's where we spend it.

### 6. Global is the default, not the edge case

Someone in every timezone, currency, and jurisdiction is using this right now. They're not an edge case to handle later — they're the base case. We design for all of them, not for the demo.
