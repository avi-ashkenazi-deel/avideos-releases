# Vision sprint pitch: deel.design

**The problem**
Designers outside Deel can't see our work. The product sits behind a login, the team lives in dozens of countries, and our careers page looks like everyone else's. We're competing for great designers without showing them why they'd want to join.

**The bet**
A public home for Deel Design will help us hire better designers and give the team something to be proud of. The story is "craft at speed": real work shipped fast by people who live all over the world.

**Where we're starting**
We aren't starting from zero. We have a brief, a positioning, draft brand principles, and a working homepage prototype.

**What we'll do in 2 days**
- **Day 1:** Build the site. Turn the prototype into a real website with our places, photos, shipped work, and first stories.
- **Day 2:** Polish it and present it to leadership, along with a simple plan for launch and upkeep.

**What we won't do**
No full design system, no newsletter, and no content schedule we'd have to keep up.

**The ask**
Two days and a slot with leadership. We'll leave with a working site and a clear go or no-go for launch.
